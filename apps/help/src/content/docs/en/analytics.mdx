**Analytics** shows how your agents are being used. It's a read-only view of
**conversation metrics**: how many conversations happen, how engaged users are, and,
for each agent, which **conversation categories** come up most.

You open the workspace dashboard from **Analytics** in the left sidebar of
**Studio**. It covers the whole **workspace** and can be filtered by **agent** and
**date range**. Each conversation agent also has its own Analytics view, opened from
the **Analytics** card on the agent's page. Categories are shown there, agent by
agent.

> **Optional feature — ask to have it enabled.** Analytics is turned on per
> workspace. If you don't see **Analytics** in the Studio sidebar, the feature isn't
> enabled for your workspace yet — contact the Bayes team to request it.

## What the dashboards show

The **workspace dashboard** has a **filter row** at the top right and two charts:

- **Conversations per day**: the number of conversational sessions started each day,
  with **Total conversations** as the headline number.
- **Avg. user questions per session**: the average number of user messages per
  session, per day.

The **agent's Analytics view** shows the same two charts for that agent, then
**Conversations by category**: a list of the agent's categories ranked by
conversation volume over the period. Each row shows:

- the category name, with a bar comparing its volume to the other categories;
- **Conversations**: the number of conversations in that category;
- **Share**: the share of the period's conversations that fall in that category. A
  conversation can have several categories, so the shares can add up to more than
  100%;
- **Trend**: a small curve of the category's daily volume over the period.

Conversations without a category are grouped in an **Uncategorized** row, shown in
grey at the bottom of the list. Click any row to open its daily volume as a bar
chart.

The charts read the sessions your agents have had, including conversations held in
the embedded chat, so a brand-new workspace with no conversations yet will show empty
or zero data.

## Step by step

### 1. Open Analytics

In **Studio**, open the left sidebar and select **Analytics**. The dashboard opens
for your whole workspace.

### 2. Read the filter row

At the top right you'll find two controls: an **agent filter** and a **Date range**
picker. They apply to every chart below.

### 3. Filter by agent

Use the agent dropdown to focus on a single agent, or keep **All agents** to see the
whole workspace combined.

### 4. Choose the period

Open the **Date range** picker and pick a period (it defaults to the last 7 days).
All charts update to that range.

### 5. Conversations per day

The **Conversations per day** chart shows how many conversational sessions started
each day, with **Total conversations** as the headline number for the period.

### 6. Avg. user questions per session

The **Avg. user questions per session** chart shows the average number of user
messages per session, a measure of how much back-and-forth each conversation
involves.

### 7. Open a conversation agent

Categories are read agent by agent. In the **Agents** list of the sidebar, click a
conversation agent. Its page opens with three cards: **New Chat**, **Analytics** and
**Feedback**.

### 8. Open the agent's Analytics

Click the **Analytics** card (*Conversation metrics for this agent.*). The **Agent
analytics** page opens, with the agent's name and type under the title and a **Date
range** picker at the top right. It shows **Conversations per day** and **Avg. user
questions per session** for this agent.

### 9. Read Conversations by category

Scroll down to **Conversations by category**. The categories are ranked from the
most to the least frequent, with their **Conversations**, **Share** and **Trend**.
**Uncategorized** sits apart at the bottom. Click a row to open that category's daily
volume below it; hover a bar to see the date and the number of conversations. Click
the row again to close it.

## Tips

- Start with **All agents** on the workspace dashboard for the big picture, then
  filter to one agent to spot which agents drive the most activity.
- Compare periods by changing the **Date range**, for example this week and then last
  week.
- On an agent's view, a category whose **Trend** rises at the end of the period is
  worth a look even if it is low in the ranking.
- A large **Uncategorized** row means the agent's categories don't cover what users
  talk about. Review them in [Admin](/en/workspace-admin).

## Troubleshooting

- **I don't see Analytics in the sidebar** — Analytics is an optional feature; it
  may not be enabled for your workspace. Ask your workspace **Owner** or an **Admin**
  if it's missing.
- **I don't see the Analytics card on an agent's page** — the card only appears on
  **conversation** agents, and only for people who can manage the agent.
- **I don't see Conversations by category on the agent's view** — the list only
  appears once conversation categories are enabled on that agent. Enable them on
  the agent's **Conversation categories** tab.
- **The charts are empty** — there may be no conversations in the selected period.
  Widen the **Date range**, or check that the selected agent has had sessions.
- **Everything is Uncategorized** — sessions are only sorted into categories once
  the agent has categories enabled; earlier sessions stay **Uncategorized**.
- **The dates look English** — chart date labels are currently shown in a fixed
  English format regardless of the interface language.
