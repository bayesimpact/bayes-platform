An administrator gives you access by adding your **email address** to a
**workspace**, an **agent** or a [**review campaign**](/en/review-campaigns). Access
is granted right away: there is no invitation email and nothing to accept. You just
sign in and find what you were given.

This guide covers your first sign-in: signing in with your organization's account,
accepting the **terms**, then finding your workspaces on the home page.

## How access works

- You sign in with **your organization's account**, the same one you use for your
  other work tools. The platform has no separate password.
- An administrator adds you by email from a **Members** page or a campaign's
  **Participants** tab. Until your first sign-in, they see a **Never signed in**
  badge next to your email.
- Sign in with the **same email address** the administrator used. That's how the
  platform links your account to the access you were given.
- The platform doesn't email you. Ask the person who added you for the platform's
  address if you don't have it.

## Step by step

### 1. Accept the terms

Open the platform and sign in with your organization's account. On your first
sign-in, a **Terms & Compliance** screen asks you to review and accept a few
documents. Tick:

- **I accept the General Conditions of Service (required)**
- **I accept the Privacy Policy (required)**
- **I accept the AI Systems Usage Policy (optional)**

Use the **Open** link next to each to read the document. The **Accept and continue**
button stays disabled until both **required** boxes are ticked. Click it to enter the
platform.

### 2. Find your workspaces

You land on the home page (**Welcome, …!**). It lists every workspace you have access
to, grouped under its **organization**. Use the **Search** box at the top right to
filter them when you have many. Being added to an agent or a campaign also gives you
access to its workspace, so it shows up here too.

### 3. Open a workspace

Each workspace card has an open button named after your main app (for example
**App** or **Studio**). Click it to enter the workspace. If you can use several apps,
a chevron next to it lists them all. See [Apps overview](/en/apps-overview) for what
each app does.

### 4. Rename your organization (owners and admins)

If you **own or administer** an organization, its card shows a **pencil** next to its
name. Click it to rename the organization. Members without that role don't see the
pencil.

### 5. If you see "No access yet"

If nobody has added you yet, you see a **No access yet** page instead of the home
page: *Your account is active, but no one has given you access to a workspace. Ask
your administrator to add you.* Your account works; it simply has no workspace yet.
Ask your administrator to add your email, then sign in again. Use **Log out** to
leave the page.

## Tips

- Give your administrator the exact email address of your organization's account, so
  the access they add matches the account you sign in with.
- You can **edit your display name** later from the user menu. See
  [Manage your profile](/en/manage-your-profile).
- Not sure which app to open? See [Apps overview](/en/apps-overview).

## Troubleshooting

- **The Accept and continue button is greyed out.** Tick both the **General
  Conditions** and the **Privacy Policy**. The AI usage policy is optional.
- **I see No access yet.** Nobody has added your email yet, or they added a
  different address. Check with your administrator which email they used, then sign
  in again once they've added you.
- **A workspace I expected is missing.** Access is given per workspace, agent or
  campaign. Ask the workspace admin to add you from **Members**, or the campaign
  owner to add you in **Participants**.
- **I see Sign-in failed.** Your organization's sign-in service refused the
  sign-in. Click **try again**, or **log out** and sign in again. If it keeps
  happening, send the message shown on the page to the administrator of the
  platform.
- **I see Create organization instead.** Your account can create its own
  organization and hasn't been added anywhere yet. Create one, or ask to be added
  to an existing workspace.
