An administrator invites you by your **email address** to a **workspace**, an
**agent** or a [**review campaign**](/en/review-campaigns). The invitation waits for
you in the platform: you sign in, accept it on the home page, and get access.

This guide covers your first sign-in: signing in with your organization's account,
accepting the **terms**, accepting your **invitations**, then finding your workspaces
on the home page.

## How access works

- You sign in with **your organization's account**, the same one you use for your
  other work tools. The platform has no separate password.
- An administrator invites you by email from a **Members** page or a campaign's
  **Participants** tab. The invitation gives no access yet: you get it when you
  accept.
- Sign in with the **same email address** the administrator used. Your identity
  provider must have verified that address. That's how the platform finds the
  invitations meant for you.
- The platform doesn't email you. Ask the person who invited you for the platform's
  address if you don't have it.

## Step by step

### 1. Accept the terms

Open the platform and sign in with your organization's account. On your first
sign-in, a **Terms & Compliance** screen asks you to review and accept a few
documents. Tick:

- **I accept the General Conditions of Service (required)**
- **I accept the Privacy Policy (required)**
- **I accept the AI Systems Usage Policy (optional)**

Use the **Open** link next to each to read the document. The **Accept and continue**
button stays disabled until both **required** boxes are ticked. Click it to enter the
platform.

### 2. Accept your invitations

You land on the home page (**Welcome, …!**). If someone invited you, a **Pending
invitations** block sits at the top: *Accept an invitation to get access.* Each
invitation is a card with:

- a badge for what you're invited to: **Project** (a workspace), **Agent** or
  **Review campaign**;
- its name;
- the organization and your role (for example *Bayes Impact Demo · Role: admin*). Agents and
  campaigns also show their workspace.

Click **Accept** to get access right away: the workspace then appears on the home
page. Click **Decline** if you don't want it; the invitation disappears. An
invitation to a review campaign can only be accepted while the campaign is active.

### 3. Find your workspaces

Below, the home page lists every workspace you have access to, grouped under its
**organization**. Use the **Search** box at the top right to filter them when you
have many. Accepting an invitation to an agent or a campaign also gives you access
to its workspace, so it shows up here too.

### 4. Open a workspace

Each workspace card has an open button named after your main app (for example
**App** or **Studio**). Click it to enter the workspace. If you can use several apps,
a chevron next to it lists them all. See [Apps overview](/en/apps-overview) for what
each app does.

### 5. Rename your organization (owners and admins)

If you **own or administer** an organization, its card shows a **pencil** next to its
name. Click it to rename the organization. Members without that role don't see the
pencil.

### 6. If you see "No access yet"

If you have no workspace and no pending invitation, you see a **No access yet** page
instead of the home page: *Your account is active, but you have no workspace and no
pending invitation. Ask your administrator to invite you.* Your account works; it
simply has nothing to open yet. Ask your administrator to invite your email, then
sign in again. Use **log out** to leave the page.

## Tips

- Give your administrator the exact email address of your organization's account, so
  the invitation matches the account you sign in with.
- Declined an invitation by mistake? Ask the administrator to invite you again.
- You can **edit your display name** later from the user menu. See
  [Manage your profile](/en/manage-your-profile).
- Not sure which app to open? See [Apps overview](/en/apps-overview).

## Troubleshooting

- **The Accept and continue button is greyed out.** Tick both the **General
  Conditions** and the **Privacy Policy**. The AI usage policy is optional.
- **I see No access yet.** Nobody has invited your email yet, they invited a
  different address, or you already declined the invitation. Check with your
  administrator which email they used, then sign in again once they've invited you.
- **I don't see the invitation I expected.** Make sure you signed in with the invited
  email address, and that your identity provider has verified it. An invitation to a
  campaign that isn't active yet can't be accepted; ask the campaign owner to
  activate it.
- **A workspace I expected is missing.** Access is given per workspace, agent or
  campaign, and only once you accept. Check the **Pending invitations** block, or ask
  the workspace admin to invite you from **Members**, or the campaign owner to invite
  you in **Participants**.
- **I see Sign-in failed.** Your organization's sign-in service refused the
  sign-in. Click **try again**, or **log out** and sign in again. If it keeps
  happening, send the message shown on the page to the administrator of the
  platform.
- **I see Create organization instead.** Your account can create its own
  organization and hasn't joined any workspace yet. Create one, or ask to be invited
  to an existing workspace.
