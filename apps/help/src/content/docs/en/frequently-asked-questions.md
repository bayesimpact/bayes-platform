## How do I reset my password?

The platform has no password of its own: you sign in with your organization's
account. Reset it the way your organization usually does, for example with the
**Forgot password** link on its login screen when it offers one. If you are not
sure, ask your IT team or your administrator.

## Why can't I see a workspace?

Access is granted per workspace, once you accept the invitation. Check the
**Pending invitations** block on the home page. If there is nothing there, ask a
workspace admin to invite you as a member.

## Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is restricted to the
members of your workspace.

## Who do I contact for more help?

Reach out to your workspace administrator, or contact the Bayes team if the issue
persists.
