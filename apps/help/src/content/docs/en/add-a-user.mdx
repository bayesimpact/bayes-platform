Inviting a user gives someone access to **one of your agents**. You invite people by
their **email address**. Each one gets a **pending invitation**, which they accept in
the platform after signing in. They then become a **member** of that agent and can
use it in the app.

This is different from inviting a workspace collaborator: see
[Invite an admin](/en/add-an-admin) to give access to the whole **workspace**. Here
you grant access to a **single agent**, from that agent's own **Members** page.

## How agent members work

Each agent has its own list of members. You enter one or more **email addresses** and
click **Invite**. Each person gets a pending invitation, listed under **Pending
invitations** below the members grid. The platform sends no email: copy each
person's **invitation link** and send it to them yourself. They sign in with your **organization's account** and accept the invitation on the
home page. They then appear in the members grid with the role **member**. You can
delete a pending invitation or remove a member at any time.

> **Roles are shown but not chosen here.** The **Invite users** dialog asks only for
> email addresses; there is no role picker. The role labels on the cards (**owner** /
> **admin** / **member**) are the platform's built-in role names.

## Step by step

### 1. Open the agent

In **Studio**, open the agent you want to give access to, either from the **Agents**
group in the left sidebar or from its card on the workspace overview.

### 2. Open the agent's Members page

On the agent's page, click **Members** in the top-right actions (next to **App** and
**Edit**). The page is titled **Members of** your agent, with the description *Invite
users who can use your agent.* It lists everyone who already has access, with the
**Invite users** card at the end of the grid.

### 3. Open the Invite users dialog

The **Invite users** card reads *Enter the email addresses of the people who should
use this agent. They accept the invitation after signing in.* Click its **Invite**
button to open the **Invite users** dialog.

### 4. Add the email address

1. Type the person's address in the **Email address** field (placeholder **Enter
   email address**).
2. Click **Add** next to the field, or press **Enter**. This only puts the address in
   the list: it appears as a chip below the field. Repeat to invite several people at
   once; remove a chip with its **✕** if you added one by mistake.

An invalid address shows **Invalid email address**, and an address already in the
list shows **Email already added**.

### 5. Send the invitations

Click **Invite** at the bottom of the dialog. The dialog closes and a **People
invited** notification confirms it. The button stays disabled until at least one
address is in the list. Someone who is already a member, or already invited, is
skipped.

### 6. Follow pending invitations

Each person now appears under **Pending invitations**, below the members grid: *People
invited to this agent who have not answered yet.* Each card shows the email and when
it was sent (for example *Invited 2 minutes ago*). They have no access yet. The section reminds you:
*Send each person their invitation link. They must sign in, or sign up, with the exact
email address shown on their invitation, otherwise they will not see it.*

Click the **link** icon (**Copy invitation link**) on a card. An **Invitation link
copied** notification confirms it. Send the link to that person: it opens the platform
with their email already filled in on the sign-in screen, where they can also sign
up if they have no account yet. They then accept the invitation (see
[Sign in and accept your invitations](/en/sign-in-for-the-first-time)).

### 7. See the new member

Once a person accepts, their invitation leaves the pending list and they appear in the
members grid with their name and the role **member**. They can use the agent, and
see its workspace in the app. If they decline, the invitation simply leaves the
pending list.

### 8. Delete an invitation or remove a member

To cancel an invitation that hasn't been answered, click the **trash** icon (**Delete
invitation**) on its card. The **Delete invitation?** dialog warns that the person
will no longer be able to accept it. Click **Confirm**.

To remove someone who already joined, click the **trash** icon on their card. A
confirmation dialog (**Remove *name*?**) explains the member will be removed from the
agent and that the action **cannot be undone**. Click **Remove** to confirm. You
can't remove the agent **owner** or yourself.

## Tips

- Double-check each **Email address** before clicking **Invite**: the invitation goes
  to exactly what you type, and it must match the email of the person's organization
  account.
- Invite several people in one go by adding multiple email chips before clicking
  **Invite**.
- Agent members are scoped to that agent only. To give someone access to the whole
  workspace instead, use [Invite an admin](/en/add-an-admin).

## Troubleshooting

- **The invitation stays pending.** The person hasn't accepted yet. Remind them to
  sign in with their organization's account, using the email you invited, and to
  click **Accept** on the home page.
- **The person sees No access yet.** They signed in with a different email than the
  one you invited, or their identity provider hasn't verified it. Delete the
  invitation and invite the address of their organization account.
- **I invited the wrong address.** Click the **trash** icon on that pending invitation
  and confirm, then invite the correct email.
- **The person declined by mistake.** Invite them again; a new pending invitation
  appears.
- **Some cards show Never signed in.** These people were given access before
  invitations existed and haven't signed in since. They already have access and
  only need to sign in.
- **I can't remove a member.** The **trash** icon isn't available for the agent
  **owner** or for your own account.
- **I don't see the Members button.** You need management access to the agent; ask
  the agent **owner** or a workspace **admin** if it isn't visible.
