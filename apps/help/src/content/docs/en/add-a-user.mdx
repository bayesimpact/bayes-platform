Adding a user gives someone access to **one of your agents**. You add people by
their **email address**, and they get access right away: they become a **member** of
that agent and can use it in the app, with no invitation to accept.

This is different from adding a workspace collaborator: see
[Add an admin](/en/add-an-admin) to give access to the whole **workspace**. Here you
grant access to a **single agent**, from that agent's own **Members** page.

## How agent members work

Each agent has its own list of members. You enter one or more **email addresses** and
click **Give access**. Each person immediately appears in the members grid with the role
**member**. They sign in with your **organization's account** (the platform sends no
email, so let them know). Until their first sign-in, their card shows a **Never
signed in** badge; after it, their name fills in and the badge disappears. You can
remove a member at any time.

> **Roles are shown but not chosen here.** The **Add users** dialog asks only for
> email addresses; there is no role picker. The role labels on the cards (**owner** /
> **admin** / **member**) are the platform's built-in role names.

## Step by step

### 1. Open the agent

In **Studio**, open the agent you want to give access to, either from the **Agents**
group in the left sidebar or from its card on the workspace overview.

### 2. Open the agent's Members page

On the agent's page, click **Members** in the top-right actions (next to **App** and
**Edit**). The page is titled **Members of** your agent, with the description *Add
users who can use your agent.* It lists everyone who already has access, with the
**Add users** card at the end of the grid.

### 3. Open the Add users dialog

The **Add users** card reads *Enter the email addresses of the people who should use
this agent.* Click its **Add** button to open the **Add users** dialog.

### 4. Add the email address

1. Type the person's address in the **Email address** field (placeholder **Enter
   email address**).
2. Click **Add** next to the field, or press **Enter**. This only puts the address in
   the list: it appears as a chip below the field. Repeat to add several people at once; remove a chip with its
   **✕** if you added one by mistake.

The hint under the field reminds you: *People get access right away. They sign in
with your organization's account.* An invalid address shows **Invalid email
address**, and an address already in the list shows **Email already added**.

### 5. Give access

Click **Give access** at the bottom of the dialog. The dialog closes and a **Members added**
notification confirms it. The button stays disabled until at least one address is in
the list.

### 6. See the new member

Each person appears in the members grid right away, with their email, the role
**member** and a **Never signed in** badge. They already have access to the agent,
and to its workspace in the app. Tell them to sign in with their organization's
account (see [Sign in for the first time](/en/sign-in-for-the-first-time)). Someone
who was already a member is skipped.

### 7. Check who has signed in

Once a person signs in for the first time, their name appears on the card and only
their role remains (for example **member**).

### 8. Remove a member

To remove someone, click the **trash** icon on their card. A confirmation dialog
(**Remove *name*?**) explains the member will be removed from the agent and that the
action **cannot be undone**. Click **Remove** to confirm. This is also how you take
back access from someone who never signed in. You can't remove the agent **owner**
or yourself.

## Tips

- Double-check each **Email address** before clicking **Give access**: access goes to exactly
  what you type, and it must match the email of the person's organization account.
- Add several people in one go by adding multiple email chips before clicking
  **Give access**.
- Agent members are scoped to that agent only. To give someone access to the whole
  workspace instead, use [Add an admin](/en/add-an-admin).

## Troubleshooting

- **The card still shows Never signed in.** The person hasn't signed in yet. Remind
  them to sign in with their organization's account, using the email you added.
- **The person sees No access yet.** They signed in with a different email than the
  one you added. Remove the wrong entry and add the address of their organization
  account.
- **I added the wrong address.** Click the **trash** icon on that card and confirm,
  then add the correct email.
- **I can't remove a member.** The **trash** icon isn't available for the agent
  **owner** or for your own account.
- **I don't see the Members button.** You need management access to the agent; ask
  the agent **owner** or a workspace **admin** if it isn't visible.
