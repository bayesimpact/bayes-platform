Inviting an admin gives someone access to **manage your workspace** with you. You
invite people by their **email address**. Each one gets a **pending invitation**,
which they accept in the platform after signing in. Access starts when they accept.

Members are managed from the **Members** page in the left sidebar of **Studio**.
Members belong to the **workspace**: everyone who joins can work on the workspace's
agents and resources.

## How workspace members work

You enter one or more **email addresses** and click **Invite**. Each person gets a
pending invitation, listed under **Pending invitations** below the members grid. If your platform is set up to send emails, each
person receives their **invitation link** by email. Otherwise, copy the link and send
it to them yourself. They sign in with your **organization's
account** and accept the invitation on the home page. They then appear in the members
grid as an **Admin** of the workspace. Each card shows a **role badge**: **Owner** for
the workspace owner, **Admin** for the people who joined. You can delete a pending
invitation or remove a member at any time.

> **Roles are shown but not chosen here.** The **Invite admins** dialog asks only for
> email addresses; there is no role picker. The **Owner** / **Admin** labels on the
> cards are the platform's built-in role names and stay in English in every language.

## Step by step

### 1. Open Members

In **Studio**, open the left sidebar and select **Members** (or click the **Members**
card on the workspace overview). The page is titled **Members of** your workspace,
with the description *Invite members to your workspace to collaborate together.* It
lists everyone who already has access. Use the **Search by name or email…** box at
the top right to find someone.

### 2. Open the Invite admins dialog

At the end of the grid, the **Invite admins** card reads *Enter the email addresses
of the people who should manage this workspace. They accept the invitation after
signing in.* Click its **Invite** button to open the **Invite admins** dialog.

### 3. Add the email address

1. Type the person's address in the **Email address** field (placeholder **Enter
   email address**).
2. Click **Add** next to the field, or press **Enter**. This only puts the address in
   the list: it appears as a chip below the field. Repeat to invite several people at
   once; remove a chip with its **✕** if you added one by mistake.

An invalid address shows **Invalid email address**, and an address already in the
list shows **Email already added**.

### 4. Send the invitations

Click **Invite** at the bottom of the dialog. The dialog closes and a **People
invited** notification confirms it. The button stays disabled until at least one
address is in the list. Someone who is already a member, or already invited, is
skipped.

### 5. Follow pending invitations

Each person now appears under **Pending invitations**, below the members grid: *People
invited to this project who have not answered yet.* Each card shows the email and
when it was sent (for example *Invited 2 minutes ago*). They have no access yet. The section reminds you:
*Send each person their invitation link. They must sign in, or sign up, with the exact
email address shown on their invitation, otherwise they will not see it.*

If your platform sends emails, each person already received their link, and the
notification reads **Invitations sent by email**. Otherwise, or to send a link again,
click the **link** icon (**Copy invitation link**) on a card. An **Invitation link
copied** notification confirms it. Send the link to that person: it opens the platform
with their email already filled in on the sign-in screen, where they can also sign
up if they have no account yet. They then accept the invitation (see
[Sign in and accept your invitations](/en/sign-in-for-the-first-time)).

### 6. See the new member

Once a person accepts, their invitation leaves the pending list and they appear in the
members grid with their name and the **Admin** badge. The workspace **Owner** has a
crown badge, admins a star. Click a member's card to open their detail page and
review which agents they can access. If they decline, the invitation simply leaves
the pending list.

### 7. Delete an invitation or remove a member

To cancel an invitation that hasn't been answered, click the **trash** icon (**Delete
invitation**) on its card. The **Delete invitation?** dialog warns that the person
will no longer be able to accept it. Click **Confirm**.

To remove someone who already joined, click the **trash** icon on their card. A
confirmation dialog (**Remove *name*?**) explains the member will be removed from the
project and that the action **cannot be undone**. Click **Remove** to confirm. You
can't remove the workspace **Owner** or yourself.

## Tips

- Double-check each **Email address** before clicking **Invite**: the invitation goes
  to exactly what you type, and it must match the email of the person's organization
  account.
- Invite several people in one go by adding multiple email chips before clicking
  **Invite**.
- To give someone access to a single agent only, use [Invite a user](/en/add-a-user)
  instead.

## Troubleshooting

- **The invitation stays pending.** The person hasn't accepted yet. Remind them to
  sign in with their organization's account, using the email you invited, and to
  click **Accept** on the home page.
- **The person sees No access yet.** They signed in with a different email than the
  one you invited, or their identity provider hasn't verified it. Delete the
  invitation and invite the address of their organization account.
- **I invited the wrong address.** Click the **trash** icon on that pending invitation
  and confirm, then invite the correct email.
- **The person declined by mistake.** Invite them again; a new pending invitation
  appears.
- **Some cards show Never signed in.** These people were given access before
  invitations existed and haven't signed in since. They already have access and
  only need to sign in.
- **I can't remove a member.** The **trash** icon isn't available for the workspace
  **Owner** or for your own account.
- **I don't see the Members page.** You need access to the workspace's settings; ask
  the workspace **Owner** if it isn't visible.
