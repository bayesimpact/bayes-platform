Adding an admin gives someone access to **manage your workspace** with you. You add
people by their **email address**, and they get access right away: there is no
invitation to send or accept.

Members are managed from the **Members** page in the left sidebar of **Studio**.
Members belong to the **workspace**: everyone you add can work on the workspace's
agents and resources.

## How workspace members work

You enter one or more **email addresses** and click **Give access**. Each person immediately
appears in the members grid as an **Admin** of the workspace. They sign in with your
**organization's account** (the platform sends no email, so let them know). Until
their first sign-in, their card shows a **Never signed in** badge; after it, their
name fills in and the badge disappears. Each card shows a **role badge**: **Owner**
for the workspace owner, **Admin** for the people you add. You can remove a member
at any time.

> **Roles are shown but not chosen here.** The **Add admins** dialog asks only for
> email addresses; there is no role picker. The **Owner** / **Admin** labels on the
> cards are the platform's built-in role names and stay in English in every language.

## Step by step

### 1. Open Members

In **Studio**, open the left sidebar and select **Members** (or click the **Members**
card on the workspace overview). The page is titled **Members of** your workspace,
with the description *Add members to your workspace to collaborate together.* It
lists everyone who already has access. Use the **Search by name or email…** box at
the top right to find someone.

### 2. Open the Add admins dialog

At the end of the grid, the **Add admins** card reads *Enter the email addresses of
the people who should manage this workspace.* Click its **Add** button to open the
**Add admins** dialog.

### 3. Add the email address

1. Type the person's address in the **Email address** field (placeholder **Enter
   email address**).
2. Click **Add** next to the field, or press **Enter**. This only puts the address in
   the list: it appears as a chip below the field. Repeat to add several people at once; remove a chip with its
   **✕** if you added one by mistake.

The hint under the field reminds you: *People get access right away. They sign in
with your organization's account.* An invalid address shows **Invalid email
address**, and an address already in the list shows **Email already added**.

### 4. Give access

Click **Give access** at the bottom of the dialog. The dialog closes and a **Members added**
notification confirms it. The button stays disabled until at least one address is in
the list.

### 5. See the new member

Each person appears in the members grid right away, with their email, the **Admin**
badge and a **Never signed in** badge. They already have access: tell them to sign
in with their organization's account (see
[Sign in for the first time](/en/sign-in-for-the-first-time)). Someone who was
already a member is skipped.

### 6. Check who has signed in

Once a person signs in for the first time, their name appears on the card and only
the **role badge** remains: **Owner** (crown) for the workspace owner, **Admin**
(star) for the people you added. Click a member's card to open their detail page and
review which agents they can access.

### 7. Remove a member

To remove someone, click the **trash** icon on their card. A confirmation dialog
(**Remove *name*?**) explains the member will be removed from the project and that
the action **cannot be undone**. Click **Remove** to confirm. This is also how you
take back access from someone who never signed in. You can't remove the workspace
**Owner** or yourself.

## Tips

- Double-check each **Email address** before clicking **Give access**: access goes to exactly
  what you type, and it must match the email of the person's organization account.
- Add several people in one go by adding multiple email chips before clicking
  **Give access**.
- To give someone access to a single agent only, use [Add a user](/en/add-a-user)
  instead.

## Troubleshooting

- **The card still shows Never signed in.** The person hasn't signed in yet. Remind
  them to sign in with their organization's account, using the email you added.
- **The person sees No access yet.** They signed in with a different email than the
  one you added. Remove the wrong entry and add the address of their organization
  account.
- **I added the wrong address.** Click the **trash** icon on that card and confirm,
  then add the correct email.
- **I can't remove a member.** The **trash** icon isn't available for the workspace
  **Owner** or for your own account.
- **I don't see the Members page.** You need access to the workspace's settings; ask
  the workspace **Owner** if it isn't visible.
