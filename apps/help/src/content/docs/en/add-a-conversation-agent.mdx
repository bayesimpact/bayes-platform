A **conversation agent** is the platform's core agent type: it **chats with users**
and answers their questions. You can ground its answers in your workspace's
knowledge — the **documents** you've uploaded — and let it surface entries from your
**resource libraries** during the conversation.

Agents live in your **workspace** and are created from the **New Agent** card on the
workspace overview (or the **+** next to **Agents** in the left sidebar).
**Conversation** is the default type.

## How the editor works

Once created, the agent opens in its **editor**, a page of its own. Its left menu
shows **Back** (to leave the editor), the agent's name with *Edit Conversation Agent*,
then the sections:

- **General**, **Model**, **Sources**, **Tools** and, when your workspace has
  categories, **Conversation categories**. These sections write to the agent's
  **draft**.
- Under **More**: [**MCP Servers**](/en/mcp-servers),
  [**Orchestration**](/en/agent-orchestration) and [**Embed**](/en/agent-embed), when
  they are enabled. These settings are not part of the draft: they apply to the agent
  in production.
- At the bottom, **Manage versions** opens the [version history](/en/agent-version-history).

**There is no Save button in the draft sections.** Each change saves by itself to the
draft, a moment after you stop typing. The status at the top right of the editor
tells you where it stands: **Saving…**, then **Draft saved**. Users keep talking to
the published version until you save the draft as a version. Three settings are the
exception: the **Document Tags** and the **Resource libraries** in **Sources**, and the
**Conversation categories**, are saved on the agent itself and apply at once. A new agent has no
published version yet: it reaches its users once you click **Save version**.

## Step by step

### 1. Open the New Agent dialog

On the workspace overview, find the **New Agent** card and click **Create** (you can
also use the **+** next to **Agents** in the left sidebar).

### 2. Keep the Conversation type

In the **New Agent** dialog, under **Agent type**, keep **Conversation** (selected
by default).

### 3. Name the agent and create it

Type a **Name** (at least 3 characters), then click **Create**. Your agent is
created with sensible defaults and its editor opens on **General**.

### 4. General

Set the:

- **Name**: how the agent appears in your workspace.
- **Greeting**: the first message the agent sends when a conversation starts. The
  count next to the label shows its length and its maximum, for example *29 / 2,000
  characters*.
- **Instructions**: what defines the agent's behaviour and tone. The count next to the
  label shows its length.

Both text areas grow with their text. Once the agent has a published version and you
change the instructions, an **Advanced mode** button appears next to **Instructions**.
It shows your instructions as a diff with the published ones, and you can edit them
right in the diff. Click it again to go back to the plain field.

When your instructions declare `{{variables}}` and the option is enabled for your
workspace, a **Prompt variables** box under the instructions sets their default
values.

Each change saves by itself: the status at the top right reads **Draft saved**.

### 5. Model

Click **Model** in the menu. Under **Answer generation**, choose the **Model** and its
options, such as the **Temperature** (higher = more varied, lower = more focused) or
the **Thinking level**, depending on the model. Under **Document search**, choose the
**Embedding model** used to search your documents.

**Priority calls** (optional). On Gemini 3.x models, turn on **Priority calls** to get
a lower and more stable response time, useful for agents exposed to many users at
peak hours. Priority calls cost about 1.8x the standard token price. The toggle only
appears when the option is enabled for your workspace; contact the Bayes team to
request it.

### 6. Sources: documents

Click **Sources**. Its first section, **Documents**, sets what the agent searches to
answer. Choose the **Answer source**:

- **Answer from model knowledge only**: no documents consulted.
- **Search across all documents**: every document in the workspace.
- **Search by document tags**: only documents with the **Document Tags** you pick.

See [Documents](/en/documents) to upload and tag documents first.

### 7. Sources: resource libraries

Still in **Sources**, the **Resource libraries** section attaches the **libraries**
the agent can surface during a conversation. Click **Add library**, pick one, and it
appears as a tag. Use **Manage libraries** to open the library manager. See
[Resource libraries](/en/resource-libraries) to create them first.

### 8. Tools

Click **Tools**. Each tool has a switch:

- **Form filling**: the agent fills a structured form from the user's answers. When
  on, you define the form's fields (see [Add a form agent](/en/add-a-form-agent)).
- **Suggest follow-up questions**: after an answer, the agent suggests short questions
  the user can click. Set the **Maximum per answer** and, optionally, **Instructions**
  on when and how to write them.
- **Memory** (when enabled for your workspace): **Off**, **Ask before saving** or
  **Save automatically**.

### 9. Conversation categories

If your workspace has conversation categories, click **Conversation categories** and
tick those that apply to this agent (**Select all** adds them all). Categories
already used in this agent's conversations can't be unchecked.

### 10. Orchestration

When orchestration is enabled, click **Orchestration** under **More**. It opens in a
full page that reads *These settings are not part of the draft: they apply in
production as soon as you save.* Add **sub-agents** from **Available conversation
agents**, give each a **Tool name** and **Tool description**, and enable it. Click
**Save**, then confirm in **Apply these settings now?**. See
[Orchestration](/en/agent-orchestration).

### 11. Embed

When embedding is enabled, click **Embed** under **More**. It opens in a full page
too. Turn on **Enable embed**, set the **Appearance** (**Widget title**, **Logo URL**,
**Brand color**, **Banner text**), and copy the code under **Widget on a website**.
Click **Save**, then confirm. See [Embed](/en/agent-embed).

### 12. Save the draft as a version

When the draft is ready, click **Manage versions** at the bottom of the menu (or the
**Draft saved** status). In the version history, the draft is selected: click **Save
version**, name it if you wish, and click **Save version** again. The agent now runs
with this version, and users can reach it. See [Version history](/en/agent-version-history).

## The agent's page

Click the agent in the **Agents** list of the left sidebar to open its page. At the top
right:

- **Edit** opens the editor. Its badge tells you what the editor opens on: **Draft**
  when the agent has a draft (or was never published), or the published version, for
  example **v3**.
- The **More** button (three dots) holds **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

Under the agent's name, cards lead to its other pages: **New Chat** (the playground),
**Feedback**, **App** with a **Production** badge (*The agent as its users see it, in
its published version.*, shown once the agent is published), **Evaluation**, and
**Members** (see [Invite a user](/en/add-a-user)). Some cards only show when you can
use them.

**Duplicate an agent.** In **More**, click **Duplicate**. The **Duplicate Agent**
dialog explains that the copy gets the settings, tools, sub-agents and sources, and
starts as a draft; conversations, analytics and embed settings are not copied. Set the
**Name** (by default the agent's name followed by *(copy)*). When the agent has both a
draft and a published version, choose the **Settings to copy**: **Draft (version
*n*)** or **Published version *n***. Click **Duplicate**: the copy opens in its editor.

**Delete an agent.** In **More**, click **Delete**. The **Delete Agent** dialog asks
*Are you sure you want to delete "name"? This action is irreversible.* Click
**Delete** to confirm, or **Cancel**.

## Tips

- Write the **Instructions** in the language your users speak.
- Use **Search by document tags** to keep answers scoped to the right documents in a
  large workspace.
- Change the draft freely: users see nothing until you click **Save version**, except
  for document tags, resource libraries and conversation categories, which apply at
  once.
- **Conversation categories**, **MCP Servers**, **Orchestration** and **Embed** only
  appear when the workspace has categories or those features are enabled. You may
  see fewer sections.
- The agent's page shows what the editor opens on: its **Edit** button carries a
  **Draft** badge when the agent has a draft, or the published version (for example
  **v3**) when it has none.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Create button is disabled.** The **Name** must be at least 3 characters.
- **The agent ignores my documents.** Check the **Answer source** in **Sources**:
  "Answer from model knowledge only" consults no documents.
- **No tags to choose.** The **Search by document tags** option and the **Document
  Tags** picker only show once your documents are tagged; see
  [Documents](/en/documents).
- **The status reads Unsaved changes.** A change could not be saved. Either a value is
  invalid, and the field says why (for example *Name must be at least 3
  characters*), or the save failed, and a red box above the fields, **This change
  could not be saved**, gives the reason. Fix the value, or click **Retry**. If you
  leave the section, the **Unsaved changes** dialog asks whether to **Discard** them.
- **Users don't see my changes.** They are in the draft. Click **Manage versions**,
  then **Save version**.
- **I don't see Orchestration or Embed.** They are optional features, for conversation
  agents only. Contact the Bayes team to have them enabled for your workspace.
